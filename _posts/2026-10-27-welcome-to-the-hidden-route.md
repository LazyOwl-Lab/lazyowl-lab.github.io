Welcome to **The Hidden Route**.

This website is a personal hobby project. I created it because I'm interested in cybersecurity and want to help people stay informed about what's happening in the digital world.

Cybersecurity news can be technical, confusing, or presented in a way that creates unnecessary alarm. My aim is simple: share useful developments, explain why they matter, and make the information easier to follow.

## What you'll find here

- **Security vulnerabilities:** weaknesses in software, applications, and devices, along with available advisories and fixes.
- **Data breaches:** reports about incidents involving exposed or stolen information.
- **Cyber threats:** developments involving malware, ransomware, phishing, and other online risks.
- **Security updates:** important patches, alerts, and practical recommendations.
- **Research and discoveries:** findings that help explain how cybersecurity threats and defences evolve.

## Keeping things transparent

The Hidden Route is not a professional newsroom. It's a personal project, and I'll do my best to share accurate and useful information, but mistakes can happen.

Where possible, I'll link to original sources, official advisories, and research so readers can check the details themselves. I'll aim to distinguish confirmed facts from allegations or information that is still being investigated. If I find an error or new information changes a story, I'll do my best to correct or update it.

Some posts will summarise or discuss reporting from other organisations. I'll credit and link to those sources rather than present someone else's work as my own.

## Why this name?

The digital world has routes and connections that aren't always obvious. I chose **The Hidden Route** as a reminder to look a little deeper, follow the evidence, and understand the context behind cybersecurity news.

I'm doing this because I enjoy learning about cybersecurity and sharing what I learn with others. Whether you're a student, a technology enthusiast, or simply someone who wants to stay safer online, I hope you'll find something useful here.

Thanks for visiting.
