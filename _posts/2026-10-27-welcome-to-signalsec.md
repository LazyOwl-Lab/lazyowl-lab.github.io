Welcome to **SignalSec**!

I created SignalSec as a personal hobby project because I'm interested in cybersecurity and want to help people stay informed about what's happening in the digital world.

Cybersecurity news can sometimes be confusing, technical, or full of unnecessary alarm. My goal is simple: share useful information, highlight important developments, and make cybersecurity news easier to understand.

## What you'll find here

I'll share news and information about topics such as:

- **Security vulnerabilities:** Newly discovered weaknesses in software, applications, and devices.
- **Data breaches:** Reported incidents involving exposed or stolen information.
- **Cyber threats:** News about malware, ransomware, phishing, and other online threats.
- **Security updates:** Important patches, advisories, and practical security recommendations.
- **Research and discoveries:** Interesting findings that help us better understand cybersecurity.

## Keeping things honest

SignalSec is a hobby project, not a professional newsroom. I'll do my best to share useful, accurate information, but mistakes can happen.

Whenever possible, I'll link to original sources, official security advisories, and research so readers can check the information for themselves.

I'll also aim to distinguish confirmed facts from reports, allegations, and details that are still being investigated. If information changes or I discover an error, I'll do my best to correct it.

Some posts may summarise or discuss information originally published by other organisations. Where appropriate, I'll credit and link to those sources rather than presenting someone else's reporting as my own.

## Why SignalSec?

The internet is part of our everyday lives, and cybersecurity affects everyone — not just security professionals.

Whether you're a student, a technology enthusiast, someone who works with computers, or simply a person who wants to stay safe online, I hope you'll find something useful here.

I'm building SignalSec because I enjoy learning about cybersecurity and sharing what I learn with others.

There are no big promises. Just a personal project, a genuine interest in cybersecurity, and a desire to help people stay informed.

Thanks for visiting SignalSec.

**Less noise. More signal.**
