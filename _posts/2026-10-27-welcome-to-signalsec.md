Cybersecurity coverage moves quickly. A vulnerability is disclosed, an organisation investigates an incident, or researchers publish new evidence about a threat campaign. The important details can be hard to separate from speculation and alarm.

SignalSec is designed around a simple idea: report the useful signal, not the noise.

## What you'll find here

- **Vulnerability briefings:** CVE details, affected products, vendor advisories and mitigation steps.
- **Incident reporting:** what is confirmed about a reported breach, what remains unknown and where to find updates.
- **Threat intelligence:** evidence-led coverage of malware, ransomware and attacker techniques.
- **Security research:** explanations of technical findings and why they matter.

## How we approach a story

We aim to start with primary sources, link to original advisories and research, and distinguish verified information from claims that have not been independently confirmed. Where possible, reporting should explain who may be affected and what practical steps are recommended by the vendor or responsible authority.
